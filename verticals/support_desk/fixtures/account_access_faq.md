# Account Access FAQ

Customers reset a password from the sign-in page. Support must not ask for or store the password.

If the account is locked after five failed sign-ins, unlock it after the customer confirms the email on the invoice.

Multi-factor recovery uses the backup codes issued at enrollment.

If those codes are lost, a human agent verifies the account before turning multi-factor off.

Suspected account takeover is urgent and must not be handled with a password reset alone.
