# First Response SLA

Priority urgent covers a service outage (the product is down) or an account lockout: first response within 1 hour, any day.

Priority high covers billing questions: first response within 4 business hours.

Priority normal: first response within 1 business day.

The clock starts when the ticket is created.

If the queue misses the target, draft a follow-up that states the original time and the new time.
